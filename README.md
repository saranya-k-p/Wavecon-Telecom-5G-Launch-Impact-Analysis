## 📌 Project Overview

Wavecon Telecom recently launched its 5G services with the expectation of increasing customer adoption, driving revenue growth, and strengthening its market position.

However, early performance indicators showed mixed results, including a slight decline in revenue, a drop in active users, and a notable increase in customer churn.

This project analyses business performance before and after the 5G launch using Power BI. It provides insights into revenue trends, customer behaviour, subscription plan performance, and city-level adoption to support strategic decision-making.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Assess the revenue impact of the 5G launch.
- Identify underperforming KPIs after the launch.
- Analyse plan-wise revenue performance.
- Identify subscription plans requiring continuation, redesign, or discontinuation.
- Evaluate city-level revenue and customer performance.
- Develop data-driven business recommendations.

## 🛠️ Tools Used

## 🔍 Key Analysis & Insights

### 1. Revenue Impact

- Revenue declined by **0.50%** after the 5G launch.
- Revenue decreased from approximately ₹16.0bn to ₹15.9bn.
- Mumbai, Kolkata, and Delhi were among the highest revenue-contributing cities.
- Delhi, Hyderabad, Ahmedabad, and Chennai experienced revenue declines.
- Lucknow, Gurgaon, Raipur, Patna, and Jaipur recorded positive revenue growth.

### 2. Customer KPI Performance

- **ARPU increased by 11.05%**, indicating higher average revenue per user.
- **Total Active Users declined by 8.28%**.
- **Unsubscribed Users increased by 23.50%**, from 5.6M to 7.0M.
- Lucknow, Pune, Jaipur, and Chandigarh recorded significant increases in unsubscribed users.
- Customer churn emerged as the most concerning KPI following the 5G launch.

### 3. Plan-Wise Revenue Analysis

**High-performing plans**
- P1, P2, and P3 demonstrated strong revenue performance.
- P1 recorded the highest post-5G revenue of approximately ₹2.4bn.
- P11 also demonstrated positive performance after the rollout.

**Underperforming plans**
- P4, P5, and P6 showed declining or stagnant revenue trends.
- P7 and P13 remained relatively weak contributors.
- These plans require further evaluation and strategic attention.

### 4. Subscription Plan Evaluation

| Plans | Analysis | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| P1, P2, P3 | Strong revenue performance | Continue |
| P11 | Good post-5G performance | Continue |
| P4, P13 | Moderate and inconsistent performance | Redesign or reposition |
| P5, P6 | Stable but relatively flat performance | Redesign and evaluate |
| P7 | Sharp decline after 5G | Consider discontinuation |
| P8, P9, P10 | Discontinued after 5G launch | Review discontinued portfolio |

## 💡 Business Recommendations

1. **Customer Retention:** Strengthen retention initiatives, particularly in high-churn cities such as Lucknow and Pune.

2. **Plan Optimization:** Redesign mid-performing subscription plans to align with changing 5G customer requirements.

3. **Market Expansion:** Invest in high-potential cities to improve revenue performance and strengthen market presence.

4. **Customer Experience:** Improve network quality and service experience in underperforming cities to reduce customer migration.

5. **Performance Monitoring:** Continuously monitor revenue, ARPU, active users, and unsubscribed users to evaluate business performance.

## 📈 Conclusion

The analysis reveals that the 5G launch did not immediately translate into overall revenue growth.

Although ARPU increased by 11.05%, revenue declined by 0.50%, active users decreased by 8.28%, and unsubscribed users increased by 23.50%.

These findings highlight the importance of looking beyond revenue growth and understanding customer behaviour, churn patterns, and subscription plan performance.

The analysis provides actionable insights to support customer retention, plan optimization, and strategic business decisions.
